1. REDISTRIBUTED BINARY BUILD (tor.exe)
===============================================================================
The official Tor Expert Bundle Windows x86_64 binary (release 15.0.23, Tor 0.4.9.12)
distributed by The Tor Project is compiled with Onion Service Proof-of-Work (PoW) DoS
defenses enabled (--enable-gpl), and consequently reports coverage under the
GNU General Public License v3 (GPLv3):
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.en.html

In accordance with GPLv3 Section 6, the corresponding source code for this official
Tor build is publicly available from The Tor Project at:
- https://dist.torproject.org/torbrowser/15.0.23/
- https://gitlab.torproject.org/tpo/core/tor (tag tor-0.4.9.12)

Null Browser bundles the unmodified standalone tor.exe executable and invokes it solely
as an independent external subprocess via standard operating system communication
channels (mere aggregation under GPLv3 Section 5).
